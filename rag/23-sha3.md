# SHA-3 competition

NIST 2007-2012. 64 submissions. Finalists: BLAKE, Grøstl, JH, Keccak, Skein.
Winner 2012-10-02: Keccak (Bertoni, Daemen, Peeters, Van Assche). Sponge on a permutation. FIPS 202.
SHA-2 was not broken; SHA-3 is a different construction in reserve and for new designs.
SysEx content_sha256 is SHA-2. hashlib.sha3_256 exists in Python. Not used in stamp().
BLAKE3 (later, not a finalist as-is) is a descendant of BLAKE. Also not wired.
